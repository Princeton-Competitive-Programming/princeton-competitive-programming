picked into teams for the regional, following the draft process described in
the [qualifier page]({{ site.baseurl }}/icpc26). The first table lists the
students that participated in the qualifier, using the seeding from the
qualifier results. The second table lists the students that did not
participate in the qualifier but are still eligible to be picked into teams.
These students all share the same seed, which is lower than that of all
students that participated.
